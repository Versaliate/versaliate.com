Versux tablet

Mobile access to console programs, connected devices and Vorysken storage.

In development

This entry describes the project’s development state. These systems are not yet included in public Prototype 0.0.2.

Explore development →

How it works

Equip the tablet in one hand and open it with E. Its home screen offers Versux, Devices and Storage apps. The game world continues running while you use it; programs execute on their console or station.

Requirements

The reachable console requires basic software, Ektiven and an installed data radio adapter. The current radio range is 20 metres. Devices and storage also need suitable data connections and, for control actions, the responsible control console.

In the current prototype

Edit programs, inspect device data and open supported station views. The Storage app creates deposit and withdrawal orders. Radio carries information; resources need transport lines. Physically inserting and removing modules from your inventory remains a local action.

  • Current radio range: 20 m
  • Apps: Versux, Devices, Storage
  • Execution on the device, display on the tablet
