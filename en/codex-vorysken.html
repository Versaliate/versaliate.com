Vorysken & logistics

A hub, storage housings and modules organize resources and persistent transport orders.

In development

This entry describes the project’s development state. These systems are not yet included in public Prototype 0.0.2.

Explore development →

How it works

A Vorysken hub manages stock in connected storage housings. Each housing has four module slots. Item modules hold counts and individual items; material modules track exact quantities. Storage capacity requires suitable modules.

Requirements

The hub and housings require Ektiven. Data connections provide stock management; additional storage transport connections move resources. A distributor can connect more housings and devices. Branches share the throughput of their supply route.

In the current prototype

Deposit and withdrawal orders run in the background and are saved. An initial production loop carries resources from a cultivation machine into storage. The tablet’s Storage app provides remote access. Vorysken stores resources; Versux data storage holds code and values. Automatic crafting remains a development goal.

  • Storage housing: four module slots
  • Items and material quantities kept separate
  • Data access and resource transport kept separate
