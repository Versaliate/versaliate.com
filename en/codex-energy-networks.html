Energy & machines

Tervyss, Makavyr and Ektiven connect heat, motion and electrical supply into a production chain.

In development

This entry describes the project’s development state. These systems are not yet included in public Prototype 0.0.2.

Explore development →

How it works

A charcoal furnace produces heat energy: Tervyss. A hot-air engine converts it into mechanical energy, Makavyr, which drives a forming press or generator. The generator supplies Ektiven for electrical machines and devices.

Requirements

Connect compatible ports with heat pipes, drive shafts or Ektiven cables. Machines require a supply and available power; additional outputs do not multiply the energy generated.

In the current prototype

Energy powers machines. Data lines carry readings and commands. Transport lines move resources. An electrical connection alone does not provide remote control or access to storage.

  • Heat: Tervyss
  • Mechanical energy: Makavyr
  • Electricity: Ektiven
