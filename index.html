Two eras to explore.

Era 1 takes you through gathering, hunting, fishing and your first shelter. Era 2 adds connected production, cultivation and a settlement progression you can complete.

  • Woodworking, clay, charcoal and ceramics
  • Production stations and quantity-based materials
  • Water logistics and primordial resource cultivation
  • Packed-clay construction, settlement objectives and new quests
Next: Era 3 Era 3 is currently being implemented. It is not included in the public 0.0.2 prototype; there is no announced release date.

Long-term vision: a framework of 17 eras. The public prototype currently covers Eras 1 and 2.

About Versaliate

One developer. Based in Switzerland.

Versaliate is the independent game label of a solo developer in Switzerland. Versalox is the main project: a world built around survival, connected systems and the satisfaction of making progress. Public prototypes let you play along as it develops.
